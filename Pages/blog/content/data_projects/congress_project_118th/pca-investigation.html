Exploring PCA Structure in Senate Voting

March 2025 · Tags: PCA, dimensionality reduction, partisanship

This is a first look at how much of Senate voting behavior can be captured by low-rank structure. I’m trying PCA to see if ideological separation shows up as a dominant component.

Initial Observations

The first two principal components already reveal a clear separation between party affiliations:

Interestingly, a few senators are located off the main party clusters — this could suggest moderate voting behavior or attendance irregularities.
