Likelihood Ratio Achieving Testing Risk

Theorem

Let \(H_0,H_1\) denote two classes of measures indicating competing hypotheses. Define \[ \alpha(H_0,H_1) := \inf \{\mathsf{TV}(P_0,P_1) : P_i \in H_i \} \] which we might refer to as the "minimal total variation distance" (or "separation") between the two classes. Then, \[ \inf_{\phi} \sup_{i} \sup_{P \in H_i} \mathbb{P}( \phi(X) \not= y ) \geq \dfrac{1 - \alpha(H_0,H_1) }{2} .\]

Proof

\[ \inf_{\phi} \sup_{i} \sup_{P \in H_i} \mathbb{P}( \phi(X) \not= y ) \] \[ \geq \inf_{\phi} \dfrac{1}{2} \left (P_0( \phi(X) \not= y ) + P_1( \phi(X) \not= y ) \right ) \] \[ \geq \dfrac{1-\mathsf{TV}(P_0,P_1) }{2} \] Letting \(\mathsf{TV}(P_0,P_1) \rightarrow \alpha(H_0,H_1)\) we have the desired result.
