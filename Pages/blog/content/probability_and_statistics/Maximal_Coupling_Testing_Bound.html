Maximal Coupling Testing Risk

Theorem

$$ \inf_{\phi} \sup_{\pi} \mathbb{P}( \phi(X_0) \not= 0 \lor \phi(X_1) \not= 1 ) \geq 1- \mathsf{TV}(P_0,P_1) .$$ where the infimum is taken over all classifiers \(\phi\) and the supremum is taken all couplings \(\pi\) of \(P_0\) and \(P_1\).

Proof

This is a trivial consequence of the coupling formulation for Total Variation. Indeed there is a coupling \(\pi\) such that \[ \mathbb{P}(X_0 = X_1) = 1 - \mathsf{TV}(P_0,P_1). \]
